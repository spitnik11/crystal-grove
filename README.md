# Crystal Grove

A tiny top-down Godot 4.7 game: collect five crystals, avoid two roaming slimes, and enter the village gate.

## Local setup

```powershell
& .\tools\setup-assets.ps1
& 'C:\Users\losth\Desktop\godot\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe' --path .
```

Arrow keys move. Collect all five crystals, then enter the house gate. Contact with a slime costs a heart. Enter restarts after win or loss.
