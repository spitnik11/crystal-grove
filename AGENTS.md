# Crystal Grove constraints

- Godot 4.7.2, 640x480 logical viewport, integer scaling, nearest filtering.
- Keep the MVP to one handcrafted map, five crystals, two slime patrols, three hearts, and one gate.
- Downloaded licensed assets are local-only under `third_party/` and must remain gitignored.
- Do not use those assets as AI input or evaluation material.
- AI generation is reserved for a later original player character after the downloaded-first MVP passes visually.
