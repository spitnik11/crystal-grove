# Local asset manifest

Runtime art is copied locally by `tools/setup-assets.ps1` from the user's licensed CraftPix library and is intentionally gitignored. Do not publish the source packs, runtime PNGs, contact sheets, or derivatives as standalone assets. Re-check the current CraftPix license before distributing a build.

These assets may be used in the game but may not be supplied to, used to train, or used to evaluate AI generation under the recorded license terms.
